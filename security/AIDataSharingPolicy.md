# AI Data Sharing Policy

AI providers are replaceable processors, not owners of the project or the user's health data.

Default AI inputs should exclude name, email, phone, address, precise location, secrets, full medical history and unrelated identity information.

Before any external AI call, the app should show the analysis packet, explain why each field is needed, allow removal when feasible, and require user confirmation.
